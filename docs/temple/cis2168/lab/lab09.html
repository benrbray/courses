Problem 1 (AdjacencyGraph In-Degree and Out-Degree)

The in-degree of a vertex v is the number of edges with v as the target. The out-degree of a vertex v is the number of edges with v as the source. Implement the degreeIn and degreeOut methods for the AdjacencyGraph class.
